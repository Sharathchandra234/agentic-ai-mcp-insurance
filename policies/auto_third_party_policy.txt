AUTO INSURANCE — THIRD-PARTY POLICY

Policy Type:
Private Car Third-Party Liability Insurance

Document ID:
POLICY-AUTO-TP-001

Effective Date:
01 January 2026

1. POLICY OVERVIEW

This policy provides third-party liability protection for eligible insured vehicles, subject to applicable policy terms, conditions, limits, and regulations.

2. THIRD-PARTY LIABILITY

Coverage may apply when the insured vehicle is legally liable for covered third-party bodily injury, death, or property damage arising from an insured event.

The policy does not provide comprehensive own-damage protection unless specifically included in the policy schedule.

3. OWN VEHICLE DAMAGE

Damage to the insured vehicle caused by:

- Collision.
- Theft.
- Fire.
- Flood.
- Storm.
- Natural calamity.

is not automatically covered under a third-party-only policy.

Separate own-damage or comprehensive coverage may be required for such protection.

4. CLAIM REPORTING

The policyholder should report an incident to the insurer as soon as reasonably possible.

Depending on the incident, the insurer may request:

- Claim form.
- Police report.
- Driving licence.
- Vehicle registration certificate.
- Accident details.
- Third-party information.
- Photographs.
- Other relevant supporting documents.

5. EXCLUSIONS AND LIMITATIONS

Coverage may be restricted where:

- The vehicle is used for an unauthorized purpose.
- The driver does not satisfy applicable licence requirements.
- Material information has been misrepresented.
- The incident falls outside applicable policy conditions.

6. CLAIM INVESTIGATION

The insurer may verify:

- Policy validity.
- Vehicle details.
- Driver information.
- Accident circumstances.
- Third-party information.
- Police documentation.
- Available supporting evidence.

7. CLAIM DECISION

Claims are assessed according to applicable policy terms, evidence, investigation findings, and authorized procedures.

Automated systems may assist claims professionals but should not independently make final claim approval or rejection decisions.
