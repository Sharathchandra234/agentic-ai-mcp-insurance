AUTO INSURANCE — COMPREHENSIVE POLICY

Policy Type:
Private Car Comprehensive Insurance

Document ID:
POLICY-AUTO-COMP-001

Effective Date:
01 January 2026

1. POLICY OVERVIEW

This policy provides comprehensive insurance protection for eligible private passenger vehicles.

The policy may provide coverage for accidental damage, theft, fire, natural calamities, and specified third-party liabilities, subject to the terms, conditions, exclusions, deductibles, and limits described in this document.

2. ACCIDENTAL DAMAGE COVERAGE

The policy covers accidental physical damage to the insured vehicle resulting from:

- Collision with another vehicle.
- Collision with a fixed or stationary object.
- Accidental overturning.
- Accidental impact with an external object.
- Damage caused during transportation of the insured vehicle following an accident.

The insurer may require photographs, repair estimates, inspection reports, and other supporting documents before processing a claim.

3. THEFT COVERAGE

The policy covers loss of the insured vehicle resulting from theft, subject to policy conditions.

For theft claims, the policyholder may be required to provide:

- First Information Report (FIR).
- Vehicle registration documents.
- Insurance policy details.
- Vehicle keys, where applicable.
- Police investigation documents.
- Any additional documents requested by the insurer.

4. FIRE AND NATURAL CALAMITY COVERAGE

Coverage may apply to damage caused by:

- Fire.
- Explosion.
- Lightning.
- Flood.
- Storm.
- Cyclone.
- Earthquake.
- Other covered natural calamities specified by the policy schedule.

Claims remain subject to applicable deductibles and policy limits.

5. THIRD-PARTY LIABILITY

The policy may provide coverage for legally enforceable third-party liabilities arising from the use of the insured vehicle, subject to applicable limits and regulations.

Third-party liability coverage does not automatically mean that every third-party claim will be payable.

6. CLAIM REPORTING

The policyholder should report an accident or loss to the insurer as soon as reasonably possible.

The insurer may request:

- Claim form.
- Photographs of vehicle damage.
- Driving licence.
- Vehicle registration certificate.
- Repair estimate.
- Repair invoices.
- Police report where applicable.
- Other documents relevant to the claim.

7. CLAIM INSPECTION

The insurer may appoint a surveyor or claims representative to inspect the vehicle and assess the reported damage.

Repair work should follow the insurer's applicable claims process.

The insurer may request additional inspection where the reported damage and supporting evidence require further verification.

8. EXCLUSIONS

The following situations may be excluded or restricted depending on the applicable policy terms:

- Normal wear and tear.
- Mechanical or electrical breakdown not resulting from a covered event.
- Damage caused intentionally.
- Use of the vehicle for an unauthorized purpose.
- Driving without a valid licence where applicable.
- Fraudulent or materially misleading claims.
- Losses outside the geographical or policy coverage limits.

9. DEDUCTIBLE

A deductible may apply to eligible claims.

The deductible amount is determined by the policy schedule and applicable policy terms.

10. CLAIM ASSESSMENT

Claim assessment may consider:

- Circumstances of the incident.
- Policy validity.
- Coverage applicable to the event.
- Vehicle condition.
- Damage assessment.
- Repair estimates.
- Supporting documents.
- Previous claim information where relevant.
- Potential inconsistencies requiring investigation.

11. FRAUD AND MISREPRESENTATION

The insurer may investigate claims where information appears inconsistent, incomplete, materially misleading, or suspicious.

Examples of indicators requiring additional investigation may include:

- Significant inconsistencies between the reported incident and physical damage.
- Unusual claim timing.
- Repeated claims with similar circumstances.
- Unsupported repair costs.
- Inconsistent customer or vehicle information.

A risk indicator or investigation flag does not by itself establish fraud.

12. CLAIM DECISION

Coverage and claim decisions must be based on applicable policy terms, evidence, investigation findings, and authorized claims procedures.

An AI system or automated risk score should not independently approve or reject a claim.

Human claims professionals remain responsible for final claim decisions.s
